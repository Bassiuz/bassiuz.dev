Artificial Intelligence in my software

I use Artificial Intelligence while developing my software. This piece of technology brings a huge acceleration and is a good tool to broaden my knowledge. AI will never be a replacement for creativity, is no reason to not be critical on software security and usability and never has the upper hand in making technical choices. This manifest describes the choices I make every time I decide to use AI in what I develop.

With the growing use of AI in the world in making basically everything there is an understandable resistance, something I feel myself too. I can't go outside without seeing the same tasteless AI posters in the street. The same text styles, whiskers around titles and obvious fake people or products on it. AI slop is everywhere around us and it's a very natural reaction to reject everything regarding AI. Also in the field of Software Development is a boom in AI slop. On every field, every hobby, every game there are new tools that swarm the field. Everybody can now write a piece of software, put it somewhere for people to use and then just see what happens. Security in software is a real concern, and for a user it's an investment to start using a new software tool. If they find out later that it's not built well, or is quickly discontinued, it is a big disappointment.

I have more than a decade of experience in writing software within multiple disciplines, and would be capable of building a nice product without the use of AI. However I think that this all or nothing approach is a real waste. There are things that AI is really good at and it's a missed opportunity to not use this as an extent of your own skills. But it's also not more than that, it should be used as an extent of one's skills. In all those years I've been doing software development, I think I got a good grip on what disciplines still need human input.

Software security

An AI model obviously suggests code that it has seen before. That code is not secure code by definition. If you publish your code somewhere and let other people use your software, it's your responsibility to take care of the data of your users. It's not the responsibility of any AI Agent, and that's why it's important to always have a critical eye on pieces of software that can create problems on the security axis. And this critical eye is not any different than I would when reviewing code of another software developer within my team. Safety first!

Usability & reliability

An AI model will not complain if there are hundreds of new features added to a page, if features don't function well or if something breaks when you for example change timezones. How do you design a good piece of software? How do you make sure your users understand what is happening and how do you build software that works now but also works in ten years? This piece of usability is absolutely not guaranteed with AI generated software and needs a lot of finetuning from an experienced developer.

Creativity

The technology that is an LLM like we see it now is based on estimate what the most likely answer is to the question the user is asking. From all thousands if not millions comparable examples the model has seen, it proposes the answer that it has seen the most. If you ask for a pirate, you get a man with a beard, a hook and on a boat with a black flag; just because this is what the model has seen the most in its training set.

Human creativity is finding the beauty in the unexpected. The imperfection of a brushstroke on a canvas. The timing in the joke of a comedian. Finding beauty in something that nobody has thought or written down before.

Creating something completely new is just not something that AI is capable of right now. In its core it is very good in copying things that it has seen a lot and adding that together. All creativity, all the new things, has to come from a human that keeps thinking and uses their creativity. Without any creative guidance, AI just makes stuff it has seen again and again.

Critical thinking

AI Agents are real yes-men. They are proposing the answer that you are looking for most likely. It's about a validation in how you ask the question. If you ask 'I won't need an umbrella today, right?', the model will say that you indeed probably won't. But if you ask it 'Is it a good idea to take my umbrella with me?', it'll suggest you take it with you.

An AI model can never replace critical thinking. Making choices in life and being aware of things, just like my considerations here in using AI, can never be done by an AI model. No AI has been used to write this manifest, and basically every text I write is written purely by me. Writing is thinking, and critical thinking is not something that can be replaced.

Ethical considerations

There are not only technical considerations that need to be made regarding AI. The impact that AI datacenters have on the environment and the usage of training data without asking for permission or any form of compensation is reprehensible. And even with that in mind I choose to, at this moment, use AI to create new software. I do this with the consideration that I can make more impact in other choices I make in life. I think AI companies should be better regulated from the top down, and even if all consumers stop using AI, it will find its way in corporate life. So even if I decide to keep my wallet closed regarding to AI, I don't think that would change the world in a tiny bit. I hope I can make impact in the world with creating nice software that people can use.

For me it is an ongoing consideration in what I do with AI and how I use AI for those tasks. I don't blame anyone for having a different opinion, and that that is a consideration to not use my software. I hope that I've been convincing in the fact that I try my best to be responsible in my AI usage in a world that's moving very fast. I'm open to a respectful debate about my point of view, and will try to be as transparent as possible about this.

All in all, I hope that my software can make a positive impact for people that use it, and that I can keep doing this in a responsible way.
